Support

Questions, feedback or a text correction? We would love to hear from you.

Getting started

Internet is needed for your first library download. After that, downloaded texts stay available offline. The library refreshes when you open the app, and you can pull down on the home screen to search.

Common questions

  • Change between Devanagari and English. Tap the three dots in a reader to change the text size and switch between Devanagari and English.
  • Read in larger text. Rotate your phone for verse cards, then swipe between verses.
  • Save a verse. Tap the bookmark beside any verse. Long-press and drag in Bookmarks to reorder, or swipe to remove.
  • Print or share a PDF. Tap the three dots in a reader and choose a printing option.
  • See the English meaning. Meanings appear where they are available for a text.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
