Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Your privacy

The Smaran app does not require an account and includes no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

On your device

Downloaded texts, reading positions, bookmarks, language, and font settings are saved on your device. The Smaran app does not upload your reading history, bookmarks or settings. Removing the app removes its local data; device backups may retain copies.

Library updates

The app connects to a Google-hosted service to download published texts. This sends ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to Google, which may process it under its privacy policy.

Feedback and contact

Feedback opens your email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. Your email address and any information you include are shared with the developer and email providers to handle your feedback. Avoid sending sensitive information.

Contact: smaran@kewalshah.com

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Changes

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated.
